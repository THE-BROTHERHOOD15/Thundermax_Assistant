SME 2026-09-16: ThunderMax AFR is signed i16 stride 4 from 0x1981. Scale 50 raw=1.00 AFR (high) from AFR-correction pair 50 cells of -50 raw. Landmark 0b00100000=32 tails the band on 225718 0x84 files at 0x01BB5, not a fixed offset on 214470. tmax read dumps AFR; tmax read --vs diffs in AFR points; tmax read --propose emits TMax click-list. Never hex-edit. Axis (RPM/TPS) still unconfirmed. Timing-limit remains 49 raw/deg.
